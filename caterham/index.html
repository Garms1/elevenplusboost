Caterham School 11+ entrance: how to prepare

Independent co-educational school, SurreySeptember 2027 entry

Caterham School is an independent co-educational school in Surrey. Children joining at 11+ take an online pre-test and an informal interview in November, then the EDGE assessment day in January.

This page explains each stage in plain English, with the key dates, what your child will be asked to do and a simple plan for preparing. Everything here is written in our own words from Caterham School's website, with links to the school's own pages.

At a glance

An informal interview and an online pre-test in November, then the EDGE assessment day in January. Every applicant takes part; there is no shortlist.

What is tested

  • A Maths paper. The school publishes its Maths syllabus with specimen questions.
  • An EDGE paper with a piece of writing, critical thinking and problem solving.
  • Group activities on the day, which are not marked.

After the test

  • An informal interview in November.
  • The school says the assessments are designed to show a child's potential, whatever they have or have not been taught before.

How to prepare: a simple plan

  1. Start with the Maths syllabusPrint Caterham's own Maths syllabus and specimen questions. Tick off each topic as your child becomes confident with it.
  2. Write a little every weekOne short story or description a week, checked for paragraphs, punctuation and interesting vocabulary, builds the writing the EDGE paper asks for.
  3. Practise thinking out loudCritical thinking and problem solving questions reward clear reasoning. Ask your child to explain how they reached an answer in a sentence or two.
  4. Rehearse the interviewTalk about hobbies, favourite books and school subjects. Children who have practised speaking about themselves feel far calmer on the day.
  5. Sit a timed mockA few weeks before the test, try a full timed Maths mock so the length of the paper is not a surprise.

Tips from the school's guidance

  • Work through the school's own Maths syllabus and specimen questions, ticking off each topic.
  • Practise explaining how you solved a problem in a sentence or two. That is what critical thinking questions look for.
  • Write a short story or description each week and check it for paragraphs and punctuation.
  • In the group activities, listen to others, share ideas kindly and help the team finish.

Questions parents ask

Is there a shortlist after the pre-test at Caterham?

No. Caterham says every applicant takes part in the EDGE assessment day; there is no shortlist.

What is the Caterham EDGE paper?

It includes a piece of writing, critical thinking and problem solving. On the day there are also group activities, which are not marked.

Does Caterham publish practice material?

Yes. The school publishes its 11+ Maths syllabus with specimen questions. The link is in the official links section on this page.
