Whitgift School 11+ entrance: how to prepare

Independent boys' school, South CroydonSeptember 2027 entry

Whitgift School is an independent boys' school in South Croydon. At 11+, boys sit English, Maths and Thinking Skills papers on one day in January, and those who do well are invited to an interview.

This page explains the process in plain English, with key dates, what is tested and a simple plan for preparing. It is written in our own words from Whitgift School's website, with links to the school's own pages.

At a glance

One exam day in January with English, Maths and Thinking Skills papers, then an interview for boys who do well.

Key dates

WhenWhat happens
6 Nov 2026Applications close
5 Jan 2027Entrance exam day
13 to 26 Jan 2027Interviews
12 Feb 2027Decisions sent to families

What is tested

  • English, Maths and Thinking Skills papers, all sat on the same day.
  • Some children who speak English as an additional language may be asked to sit an extra short paper.
  • The school does not publish past papers, but families can ask for sample papers in English and Maths. There is no sample for Thinking Skills.

After the test

  • Boys who do well in the papers are invited to an interview with a senior member of staff.
  • The interview is a conversation about schoolwork, hobbies and what interests him. Parents usually have a short meeting too.
  • The online application form takes about 45 minutes and has to be finished in one go, so set aside time before the closing date.

How to prepare: a simple plan

  1. Ask for the sample papers earlyWhitgift does not publish past papers, but families can request sample English and Maths papers. Try them under timed conditions.
  2. Build Thinking SkillsThinking Skills rewards careful reading and logic. Puzzles, codes and pattern questions are good practice.
  3. Keep Maths sharpRegular short practice on number, fractions, problem solving and multi-step questions makes the Maths paper feel familiar.
  4. Read and write oftenReading widely and writing short pieces each week helps with comprehension and the English paper.
  5. Prepare for the interviewHelp your child choose two or three things they really enjoy and practise talking about why.

Tips from the school's guidance

  • Ask the school for its sample papers early and try them under timed conditions.
  • Thinking Skills rewards careful reading and logic. Puzzles, codes and pattern questions are good practice, and the Verbal and Non verbal lessons here cover the same kind of thinking.
  • For the interview, help your child choose two or three things they really enjoy and practise talking about why.
  • If you are interested in a bursary, read the John Whitgift Foundation page well before the closing date.
