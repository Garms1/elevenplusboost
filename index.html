Local 11+ preparation

Made for 11+ families in Caterham, Croydon and Sutton

ElevenPlusBoost is built for families in Caterham, Croydon, Sutton, Purley, Coulsdon, Wallington, Cheam, Carshalton, Banstead and the surrounding Surrey area. It covers the entrance tests for Caterham School, Trinity School and Whitgift School, and the Sutton SET used by Sutton Grammar School, Wilson's School, Wallington County Grammar School, Wallington High School for Girls and Nonsuch High School for Girls.

Questions

Questions parents ask

Which 11+ exams does ElevenPlusBoost cover?

Caterham School (the ISEB Common Pre-Test and Caterham's own Maths paper), Trinity School in Croydon, Whitgift School, and the Sutton SET used by Sutton Grammar, Wilson's, Wallington County Grammar, Wallington High School for Girls and Nonsuch High School for Girls.

Is ElevenPlusBoost free?

Yes. ElevenPlusBoost is free to use during its preview. There is nothing to install and no sign up. Progress is saved in your own browser.

How does the Parent area help?

Parents can see readiness for each school, spot weak topics, turn questions answered incorrectly into a new test, set school style mocks and add a deadline that appears on the child's dashboard.

What subjects are included?

Maths, English, verbal reasoning and non verbal reasoning, with timed mock tests, answer papers for every finished test, bitesize lessons, vocabulary practice, writing tasks and interview practice.

Which year group is it for?

Children in Year 4, Year 5 and Year 6 preparing for 11+ entrance exams in Surrey, Croydon and Sutton.
